## Scope checks

- The project is a deterministic 3–15-session swing system, not an
  institutional data-certification or ML study.
- Exactly two readable setup cells are locked; no search or model remains.
- Charter capital, risk, slots, gross cap, stop bound, reward:risk, time stop,
  long-only rule, and doubled friction are explicit.
- The local Yahoo/current-roster cache is accepted without being mislabeled
  point-in-time or survivorship-free.
- Historical earnings reconstruction is omitted openly; the prospective book
  retains the contemporaneous safety veto.
- Retrospective evidence can select at most one forward-paper setup and cannot
  authorize live money.

- Equal-score symbol ordering, parameter changes, least-bad promotion,
  backfill, and evidence overwrite are prohibited.
- ML-v2 identities, evidence, and `STOP_INPUT` remain separate and unchanged.
